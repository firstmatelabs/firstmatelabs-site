Today

What we noticed

A&A has done the hard part for more than 40 years. You run 24/7 with light, medium and flatbed trucks, and you're trusted by the City of Long Beach, LBPD, CSULB PD and VA police. That city contract was amended as recently as August 2026. Few towing companies in the area can say that.

There's a real opportunity to make your online presence match the business. Here's what a new customer, property manager or partner sees today when they look you up:

  • Yelp: around 2.5 stars from about 178 reviews. Most low ratings come from private property impounds, which is the nature of the work: people rarely thank the company that towed their car. The good reviews often name your drivers and praise them.
  • BBB: an F rating tied to unanswered complaints. This one is very fixable.
  • Website: aatowing.net is due for a refresh. Most towing calls come from phones, and a modern, mobile-first site builds trust in seconds.
  • Email and social: a branded email address and a few active social profiles would give customers and partners more ways to find and trust A&A. Today, Yelp is the main profile people see.

None of this reflects the quality of the work. It's simply the part of the business that has been left to run itself. Each item has a clear fix, and most can be done in the first 30 days.

The plan

The plan, by service

1Dave, your always-on dispatcher (24/7 dispatch answering)

The problem: Every missed call is a tow job that goes to the next company on the list. Impound release questions take up a lot of dispatcher time, and upset callers make every shift harder.

What we do: Dave answers every call instantly, day or night, the way a good dispatcher would. He:

  • Answers impound questions by license plate: whether the car is there, what to bring and when to come.
  • Takes tow and roadside requests, captures the caller's details and texts them the address and release requirements.
  • Quotes from your Long Beach rate schedule, so prices are consistent every time.
  • Stays calm with angry callers and explains the process clearly and politely.
  • Transfers to a live dispatcher whenever you want a person on the line.
  • Speaks English and Spanish.

The outcome: Every call answered right away, day or night, so jobs aren't lost to voicemail. Fewer repeat calls about impounds, and dispatchers free to focus on the trucks.

2Property manager growth program: winning more impound accounts

The problem: Private property impound contracts with apartments, HOAs and commercial properties are steady, recurring work. Property managers pick the tow company that's easiest to work with and answers fastest when a tenant is upset.

What A&A can offer property managers:

  • Instant answers, any hour: every tow request, question and tenant complaint is answered right away, day or night, by Dave or your dispatchers.
  • Easy tow requests: request a tow online or by phone in under a minute.
  • Free signs and tenant permits: compliant tow-away signs and parking permits at no cost to the property.
  • Digital records for every tow: photos, time, location and reason for each tow, sent to the manager, so disputes are easy to settle.

How we win the accounts:

  • Target list: a researched list of Long Beach-area property management firms, apartment communities and HOAs, with the right contact at each.
  • Email and LinkedIn sequence: a short, friendly series of messages that leads with the offer above and asks for a 15-minute call.
  • Property manager landing page: one page that explains the offer and lets a manager sign up or request signs on the spot.
  • Property manager portal: online tow requests, tow records and permits in one place (see section 10).

The outcome: A steady pipeline of new PPI accounts, and happier existing ones, because managers get answers instead of voicemail.

3A new website

The problem: The current site is hard to use on a phone and doesn't answer the questions people actually have.

What we do: A fast, mobile-first site with a tap-to-call button on every page, plus:

  • An impound lookup and release page: hours, fees, what to bring, how to get there, and online payment (see section 10).
  • The property manager landing page from section 2.
  • Local pages for each city you serve, so you show up when people search nearby.

The outcome: More calls from search, fewer confused callers, and a site that matches the size of the company.

4Reputation repair, done the right way

The problem: Impound reviews pull the rating down, and unanswered reviews look like nobody is listening.

What we do:

  • Write calm, professional replies to Yelp and Google reviews, especially impound complaints. Each reply explains the law (California Vehicle Code 22658 covers private property tows) and the release process, without arguing.
  • Send a short text after each roadside or motor club job asking happy customers for a Google review. Yelp doesn't allow businesses to ask for reviews, so we focus requests on Google and simply respond well on Yelp.

The outcome: Future customers see a company that responds and explains, and new reviews start to reflect the customers you help every day. We never post fake reviews and we don't promise to remove any.

5Better Business Bureau

The problem: Unanswered complaints are the main reason for an F rating.

What we do: Help you answer the 2 open complaints, claim and complete the BBB profile, and look at whether accreditation is worth it for your contract work.

The outcome: Because unanswered complaints are the main driver, the rating can improve quickly once they're answered. The BBB sets the grade, so we can't guarantee one.

6Google Business Profile

The problem: For towing, the Google profile is often the first thing people see and the button they tap to call.

What we do: Set up the right categories, service areas and hours; add truck, fleet and yard photos; post regular updates; and answer common questions in the Q&A.

The outcome: A complete, active profile that shows up for "tow truck near me" and turns searches into calls.

7Business email on your own domain

The opportunity: The current email is due for a refresh. A branded address builds trust with property managers, dealers and agencies.

What we do: Move email to Google Workspace with addresses like dispatch@ and office@ on your domain, and carry over existing mail and contacts.

The outcome: A professional address for every contract conversation and better control over who has access.

8More B2B growth: motor clubs, partners and rotation lists

The problem: Beyond property managers, most steady towing revenue comes from networks and partners, not ads.

What we do:

  • Motor clubs and roadside networks: sign-ups with AAA, Agero, Honk and Urgently, if you're not already on them.
  • Partnerships: dealerships, body shops and local fleets that need a reliable tow partner.
  • Agency rotation lists: help applying to nearby city and agency rotation lists as they open, building on your existing city record.

The outcome: More recurring, predictable work that doesn't depend on who happens to search today.

9Light social media

The problem: With no social accounts, people have nothing to look at beyond reviews.

What we do: A simple Google, Facebook and Instagram presence: truck and fleet photos, community and road safety posts, and hiring posts for drivers (see the hiring page in section 10).

The outcome: To be honest, social rarely brings in tow calls directly. It builds trust when people look you up, and it's one of the best ways to recruit drivers.

10Modernize the yard and the customer experience

Small upgrades that save your team time at the phone and the window, and make A&A easier to deal with than anyone else in town.

Missed-call text-back

Problem:
A dropped or unanswered call can mean a lost job.
What we do:
Any missed call gets an instant text: "Sorry we missed you, reply here or call back for a tow."
Outcome:
The caller stays with A&A instead of dialing the next company.

Driver ETA texts

Problem:
"Where's my truck?" calls tie up dispatch.
What we do:
The customer gets a text with the driver's name, the truck and a live arrival estimate.
Outcome:
Calmer customers and fewer check-in calls.

Online impound lookup and payment

Problem:
Owners show up unsure what to bring or what they owe, and the line at the window backs up.
What we do:
Owners search by plate, see the fees and the documents they need, and pay online before they arrive.
Outcome:
A quick in-and-out at the window, fewer phone questions, and less friction on the calls that cause the most complaints.

Photo and condition records at hookup

Problem:
Damage claims and review disputes come down to your word against theirs.
What we do:
Drivers take timestamped photos of each vehicle at hookup, saved to that tow's record.
Outcome:
Clear proof of condition when a claim or complaint comes in.

Digital release paperwork and e-signature

Problem:
Paper release forms slow down every pickup.
What we do:
Release forms are filled and signed on a tablet or phone, with a copy texted or emailed to the owner.
Outcome:
Faster releases and clean records you can find in seconds.

Text-to-pay for roadside customers

Problem:
Taking cards on the roadside is slow and awkward.
What we do:
The driver or dispatcher sends a secure payment link by text.
Outcome:
Faster payment and less cash handling.

Online booking for scheduled tows

Problem:
Non-emergency jobs, like moving a car to a shop, still come in as phone calls.
What we do:
A simple booking form for scheduled and non-emergency tows, sent straight to dispatch.
Outcome:
Easy jobs book themselves, and dispatch plans the day ahead.

Lien notice tracking

Problem:
Keeping track of notice deadlines for each stored vehicle is easy to fall behind on.
What we do:
Each unclaimed vehicle gets a timeline with reminders for every notice and filing step.
Outcome:
Fewer missed deadlines and storage cars that move to lien sale on time.

Property manager portal

Problem:
Managers have to call to request a tow or check what happened at their property.
What we do:
Managers request tows online, see a record of every tow at their property with photos and timestamps, and manage tenant permits.
Outcome:
A reason for managers to choose A&A and stay with you.

Driver hiring page and recruiting posts

Problem:
Good tow drivers are hard to find.
What we do:
A hiring page on your site that takes applications, plus regular recruiting posts on social.
Outcome:
A steady flow of applicants when you need to add a driver.

11Optional: The A&A app, everything tied together

The problem: Photos, paperwork, payments and truck locations live in different places, or only in a driver's phone. When a claim or complaint comes in, the proof is hard to find.

What we build: A custom A&A app for drivers and dispatch that ties every tow together, from the first call to the release.

Driver app (Android and iPhone)

  • Photo and condition capture at hookup, stamped with time and GPS location.
  • A digital tow record for every job.
  • Live GPS tracking, so dispatch can see every truck in real time.
  • Job assignments and status updates from dispatch.
  • Customer e-signature and payment in the field.

QR codes on tow-away signs

  • Property managers and tenants scan to request a tow.
  • Vehicle owners scan "Was your car towed? Find it and pay online" and go straight to the online lookup and payment page.

Dispatch dashboard

  • Live map, job queue and driver status at a glance.
  • A full history of every tow with photos and timestamps, searchable by plate.

Extras

  • Automatic ETA texts to customers.
  • Driver shift and hours log.
  • Pre-trip truck inspection checklist.
  • An evidence pack for damage claims and review disputes: photos, times, location and signatures for any tow, ready in one click.
  • A storage-day counter on each impounded vehicle, showing fees as they build up.
  • Police and agency tows logged separately from private property tows, so city and LBPD reports are easy to pull.
  • Photo proof of each tow-away sign at a property.
  • A yard-location note for each vehicle, so staff can find it fast at release.
  • Feeds the property manager portal and lien notice tracking, so the records are always up to date.

Works with what you have: We can connect the app to whatever dispatch or CRM system you already use, or build the whole thing out for you. We'll find out what you use first.

The outcome: One system for every tow, from call to release, with proof for every claim.

12Optional add-ons

  • Google Local Services Ads ("Google Guaranteed"): pay-per-lead ads at the top of Google for towing and roadside calls. Ad spend is paid to Google directly.

13Ongoing reporting

A short monthly report in plain English: calls answered, jobs captured, new reviews and replies, Google profile views and calls, and progress on property manager and partner outreach. One page, with what we'll do next month.
