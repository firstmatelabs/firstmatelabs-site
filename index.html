How It Works

Two weeks from first call to first booking.

Day 1

A 30-Minute Call

We ask how calls reach you today, what callers ask for, and which calls you can't afford to miss.

Days 2–3

A Written Plan

One page: what the AI says, what it can do, and when it hands the call to a person. You see the price before you pay for setup.

Days 4–10

Build and Test

We load your services, hours and prices, then test with the questions your customers really ask. You listen to the test calls.

Days 10–14

Go Live

You forward your number. We read the first week of calls with you and fix what missed.

Every Month

Care

We review calls, update hours and offers, and send a plain monthly report. Cancel with 30 days notice.

What We Need From You

Most of it is things you already have.

  • Your services, hours and prices, as a document or a link to your site
  • Access to the calendar or booking tool you use
  • The five questions customers ask most
  • A phone number we can forward, or a new one
  • Someone who can answer our questions for 30 minutes
Your timeline depends on how fast you can send these. Integrations with older software can add days.

FAQ

Questions we get first.

What do you actually set up?

An AI receptionist on your phone line, an assistant that works inside your email and calendar, or an automation between the tools you already use. Which one depends on where you lose time or calls. The written plan says exactly what you get before you pay for setup.

Will callers know it's an AI?

Yes. Every agent says it is an AI assistant at the start of the call. Callers can ask for a person at any time, and the agent hands the call to the number you choose.

What happens when it can't answer?

It says so, takes a message with the caller's name and number, and sends it to you right away. It does not guess at prices, medical details or legal answers that you haven't given it.

How long until it's live?

Usually one to two weeks. The time depends on how quickly we get your hours, prices and calendar access, and on how old your software is.

Who owns the accounts?

You do. We build in accounts under your name where the tool allows it. If you leave, you keep the setup and we hand over the documentation.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans have no minimum term. Give us 30 days notice and we stop billing at the end of that period. The refund terms are on the Refund and Cancellation page.

Do you record calls?

The plans include call recordings and transcripts, sent to you. You choose whether recording is on, and the agent can announce it. You are responsible for following the recording laws where you operate.

What does the demo keep?

The voice demo runs on ElevenLabs. Calls are transcribed and kept for 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for what is kept and for how long.
