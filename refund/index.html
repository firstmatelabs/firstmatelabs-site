Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective: October 27, 2026

This policy applies to services bought from First Mate Labs LLC, 30 North Gould Street, Suite 70227, Sheridan, WY 82801. It is part of our Terms of Service. Your written plan (the "Plan") states the prices and what is included. If anything here conflicts with your Plan, the Plan controls.

1. Cancel any time, with 30 days' notice

There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee. You can cancel a monthly service at any time by emailing hello@firstmatelabs.com with the business name on your account. Cancellation takes effect 30 days after we receive your notice. The service keeps running until then, and you pay the monthly fee through that date. We confirm your cancellation by email within one business day. Monthly services renew automatically each month until you cancel. You do not need a phone call or a form to cancel.

2. Setup fee

The setup fee pays for the work of designing, building and testing your service.

  • Before work starts. If you cancel within 7 days of paying, and before we have held the kickoff call or begun work, we refund the setup fee in full.
  • After work starts. Once the kickoff call has happened or we have started the Plan or the build, the setup fee is non-refundable for the work already done. If you cancel before go-live, we refund the share of the setup fee for the work we have not yet done, and we hand over what we have built so far.
  • If we do not deliver. If we have your required materials and access, and we do not get you live with the scope in your Plan within 30 days of the start date we agreed, you can cancel and we refund the setup fee in full.
  • Custom plans. The Plan for a Custom order states any milestones and how the setup fee is split. If it does, those milestones decide what is refundable.

3. Monthly fees

Monthly fees are billed in advance and are not refunded for a month that has already started, except as stated in section 4. We do not charge a cancellation fee. If you cancel, you will not be billed for any month that begins after your notice period ends.

4. When we get it wrong

If we make a billing error, or the service is down or materially not working as described for more than 5 days in a month because of something on our side, tell us within 30 days. We will fix it and credit or refund the affected part of that month's fee.

5. Usage and third-party costs

Usage charges that were billed correctly (for example, voice minutes above your included amount) are not refundable. Fees you pay directly to other providers, such as your calendar, CRM or phone carrier, are not refundable by us.

6. After you cancel

When the service ends, we turn off your agent. On request we hand over your data, call records and any accounts that are in your name. We delete your client data on request, subject to what the law requires us to keep.

7. How to ask for a refund

Email hello@firstmatelabs.com with your business name and the invoice number. We reply within one business day. We issue approved refunds to the original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank may take additional time to show it.

8. Card disputes

If you see a charge you do not recognize, email us first and we will check it right away. You always keep the right to dispute a charge with your card issuer.
