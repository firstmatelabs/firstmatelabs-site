Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy applies to services bought from First Mate Labs LLC. Your written plan states the prices and what is included. If anything here conflicts with your plan, the plan controls.

1. Cancel Anytime, With 30 Days' Notice

There is no minimum term. You can cancel a monthly service at any time by emailing hello@firstmatelabs.com with 30 days' notice. The service keeps running until the end of the notice period, and you pay the monthly fee through that date. We confirm your cancellation by email.

2. Setup Fee

The setup fee pays for the work of designing, building and testing your service.

  • Before work starts. If you cancel within 7 days of paying and before we have held the kickoff call or begun work, we refund the setup fee in full.
  • After work starts. Once the kickoff call has happened or we have started the plan or build, the setup fee is non-refundable for the work already done. If you cancel before go-live, we refund the share of the setup fee for work we have not yet done, and we hand over what we have built so far.
  • If we do not deliver. If we have your required materials and access, and we do not get you live with the scope in your written plan within 30 days of the start date we agreed, you can cancel and we refund the setup fee in full.

3. Monthly Fees

Monthly fees are billed in advance and are not refunded for a month that has started, except as stated in section 4. We do not charge a cancellation fee.

4. When We Get It Wrong

If a billing error occurs, or the service is down or materially not working as described for more than 5 days in a month because of something on our side, tell us within 30 days. We will fix it and credit or refund the affected part of that month's fee. Usage charges that were billed correctly are not refundable.

5. Usage and Third-Party Costs

Usage above the included amount, and fees you pay directly to other providers (such as your calendar, CRM or phone carrier), are not refundable.

6. After You Cancel

When the service ends, we turn off your agent and, on request, hand over your data, call records and any accounts that are in your name. We delete your client data on request, subject to what the law requires us to keep.

7. How to Ask For a Refund

Email hello@firstmatelabs.com with your business name and the invoice. We answer within one business day and issue approved refunds to the original payment method within 10 business days.
