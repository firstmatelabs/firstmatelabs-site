4. Email setup Hurting replies

  • Quotes go out from a free @gmail.com address, not your own domain. Looks less established.
  • Your domain has no SPF record, so mail sent "from" it is easy to fake and likely to hit spam.
  • No DMARC policy.
  • Domain is registered but has no email set up on it.

Fix: email at your own domain (for example danny@ your domain) with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set. About 20 minutes of setup.
