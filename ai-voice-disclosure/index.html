AI and Voice Disclosure

Effective: October 27, 2026

First Mate Labs LLC builds AI voice agents and other AI assistants for businesses. This page explains, in plain words, how those agents behave. It applies to our website demos and to the agents we run for clients.

First Mate Labs LLC, 30 North Gould Street, Suite 70227, Sheridan, WY 82801 · hello@firstmatelabs.com · 702-987-8800

1. You are talking to an AI

Our voice and chat agents are artificial intelligence. They are not people. Every agent says so at the start of the conversation, for example: "Thanks for calling. I'm the business's AI assistant." An agent never claims to be human. If you ask whether you are speaking to a person, it tells you the truth.

2. You can ask for a person

You can ask for a person at any time. For a client's agent, the call is passed to the number the client chose, or the agent takes a message and the business calls back. A demo agent cannot transfer calls because the demo businesses are fictional.

3. Calls may be recorded or transcribed

  • Demo calls on our Site are transcribed. We keep the transcript for 30 days and then delete it. The demo is set not to save audio. The Site tells you this before you start, and starting the call means you agree.
  • Client calls are recorded or transcribed only if the business asked for it. When they are, the agent says so at the start of the call, for example: "This call is handled by an AI assistant and may be recorded and transcribed." If you do not want that, say so, and ask for a person.

4. Where your information goes

Your speech is sent to the voice platform that runs the agent (ElevenLabs for our demos), and the text may be passed to language-model providers it works with. We do not sell your information. The Privacy Policy lists the providers, what we keep and how to contact us.

5. What the agents can and cannot do

  • They answer common questions, book appointments, take messages and pass urgent calls to people.
  • They can make mistakes. Please check anything important with the business.
  • They do not give legal, medical, financial or other professional advice.
  • They are not an emergency service. In an emergency, hang up and call 911.
  • They are not set up to take medical records, health details or payment card numbers by voice. Please do not share them with an agent.

6. Outbound calls and texts

Our standard services answer calls that people make to a business. If a client's plan includes calls or texts that the agent starts, they go only to people who have agreed to receive them, and only in the way the law allows. The law treats AI-generated voices as artificial voices for this purpose, so those calls need the recipient's consent. Every message includes a way to opt out. Say "stop" on a call or reply STOP to a text, and it ends right away. We do not make cold calls and we do not run telemarketing campaigns.

7. Demo voices

The demo businesses (Alder Creek Dental and the others) are fictional, and the demo agents are not real offices. Demo voices are synthetic voices.

8. Questions or concerns

Email hello@firstmatelabs.com or call 702-987-8800. We answer within one business day.
