3. The voice demo

When you click Start on a demo call, your browser asks for permission to use your microphone. If you allow it, your speech is sent in real time to ElevenLabs, which runs the demo agent. ElevenLabs converts your speech to text, may pass that text to the language-model providers it works with to write the agent's replies, and sends back the agent's voice. The demo is configured not to save audio recordings. We receive a transcript and basic call data such as its length and errors.

Before you start, the Site tells you the call is with an AI and that it is transcribed. By starting the call, you agree to that. You must be 18 or older to use a demo. We keep demo transcripts for 30 days, in our own systems and in our ElevenLabs account, to fix and improve the demos, and then delete them. We do not use demo calls to identify you, and we do not use them to train AI models. ElevenLabs also handles the data under its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies, analytics and tracking

  • We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or cross-site tracking.
  • We do not currently run analytics on the Site. If we add analytics, we will use a privacy-focused tool, update this policy first, and ask for consent where the law requires it.
  • Our fonts and images load from our own site, not from third parties.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile loads only if you click Start on a demo call. It is there to stop bots and keep the demo from being overloaded, and it sends your IP address and device and browser signals to Cloudflare. We get back only a pass or fail result.
  • Do Not Track. Browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. Because we do not track you across websites or over time, we do not change how the Site works when we receive one. Other parties that run the demo (ElevenLabs, Cloudflare) are the only third parties that can collect information when you use it, and only when you start a call.
  • Global Privacy Control. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, so a Global Privacy Control signal does not change anything. We treat it as an opt-out anyway.

5. How we use information

  • To respond to you and to provide, support and bill for our services.
  • To run, test and improve the demo agents and our services.
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent abuse and fraud.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.
  • To send you service messages about your account. We send marketing email only to people who have asked for it or who are business contacts who can easily opt out. Every marketing email has a working unsubscribe link and our postal address, and we honor opt-outs within 10 business days.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use it for advertising.

7. Call recording and notice

Client voice agents tell callers they are speaking with an AI assistant. Recording and transcription are on only if the client has asked for them. When they are on, the agent tells the caller at the start of the call. Clients are responsible for any other notice or consent the law requires where their callers are located. Demo calls are transcribed as described in section 3.

8. How long we keep information

  • Correspondence and billing records: as long as needed for the relationship and for tax and legal purposes, normally up to seven years for billing records.
  • Demo transcripts: deleted after 30 days.
  • Bot-check data: kept by Cloudflare under its own policy. We keep only the pass or fail result.
  • Client call data: for the period in the client's Plan, then deleted or handed back at the client's request when the service ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Website logs: kept by our host under its own policy.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, such as encryption in transit and access controls, to protect information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

10. Your privacy rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it, by emailing hello@firstmatelabs.com. We will confirm who you are, reply within 30 days (45 if we need more time and tell you why), and will not treat you worse for asking.

  • California. California law gives residents the rights to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive information. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use sensitive information beyond what the law allows for providing the service. You can use an authorized agent to ask on your behalf.
  • Other states and countries. If you live somewhere else with privacy rights (for example Colorado, Virginia, Texas, the EU or the UK), the same email address works. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.
  • Callers to a client's agent. Contact that business directly. We will help it respond.
  • Marketing. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to us.

11. Children
