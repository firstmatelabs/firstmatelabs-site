Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy explains what personal information First Mate Labs LLC ("First Mate Labs", "we", "us") collects through firstmatelabs.com (the "Site") and our services, how we use it, and your choices. Our address is 30 North Gould Street, Suite 70227, Sheridan, WY 82801. You can reach us at hello@firstmatelabs.com.

1. What We Collect

Information you send us. If you email or call us, we receive your name, contact details, business information and whatever else you tell us. We keep that correspondence.

Billing information. When you buy a service, our payment processor (Stripe) collects your payment details. We do not store full card numbers. We keep invoices, billing contacts and payment status.

Voice demo data. When you start a demo call on the Site, your browser asks permission to use your microphone. If you allow it, your speech is sent in real time to ElevenLabs, the voice service that runs the demo agent. ElevenLabs turns your speech into text, may pass that text to the language-model providers it works with to write the agent's replies, and sends back the agent's voice. We receive a transcript of the call. The demo is set not to save audio recordings. We may also receive technical data about the call, such as its length and any errors. Demo transcripts are kept for 30 days, by us and in our ElevenLabs account, to improve and fix the demos, and then deleted. ElevenLabs handles the data under its own privacy policy. We do not use demo calls to identify you. Please do not give real personal or sensitive information to a demo agent.

Bot protection. When you click Start on a demo call, the Site loads Cloudflare Turnstile, a check that tells people apart from automated abuse so the demo can't be overloaded. Turnstile runs in your browser and sends Cloudflare technical signals such as your IP address, browser and device characteristics, and how the page was used. We get back only a pass or fail result. Cloudflare handles this data under its own privacy policy. Turnstile does not load unless you start a demo call.

Website data. Our servers and hosting provider log standard technical data, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will use a privacy-focused tool and update this policy.

Client service data. If you are a client, we process your business data and the data of your callers and customers (for example call audio, transcripts, appointment details and contact information) to run the service you bought. We handle this as your service provider and only as needed to deliver and support the service, as described in your plan.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to you and provide, support and bill for our services.
  • To run, test and improve the demo agents and our services.
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

3. Who We Share It With

We share information only with providers that help us operate, and only as needed:

  • Cloudflare, which runs the Turnstile bot check on the voice demo.
  • ElevenLabs, which runs the voice demo on the Site, and the other voice, speech and language-model providers that run client agents.
  • Telephony providers that carry calls and text messages.
  • Hosting, email, calendar and storage providers.
  • Stripe, for payments.
  • Professional advisers, and authorities when the law requires it.

If the business is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner under the same protections.

4. Call Recording and Notice

Client voice agents tell callers they are speaking with an AI. Recording is on only if the client asks for it. Clients are responsible for any notice or consent required where their callers are located. Demo calls are transcribed as described above.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep correspondence and billing records for as long as needed for the relationship and for tax and legal purposes, normally up to seven years for billing records. Demo transcripts are deleted after 30 days. Client call data is kept for the period set in the client's plan and deleted or handed back at the client's request when the service ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, such as encryption in transit and access controls, to protect information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it, by emailing hello@firstmatelabs.com. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU and UK), you may have additional rights, including to object to processing or to receive a copy of your data. We will respond within 30 days. If you are a caller to one of our clients' agents, please contact that business directly, since it decides how your data is used.

8. Children

The Site and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, tell us and we will delete it.

9. International Use

We are based in the United States, and information is processed there and in other countries where our providers operate. By using the Site you understand your information may be transferred to those countries.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version with a new effective date, and tell clients by email if a change is material.
